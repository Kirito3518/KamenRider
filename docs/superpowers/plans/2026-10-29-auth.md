## Constraints and review focus
- No password reset. No implicit email-based account merging.
- Unconfigured providers unavailable; secrets stay on server.
- Last login method cannot be unlinked; expired sessions must be rejected.
- Network errors restore controls; OAuth failures produce useful feedback.
- Demo rental data must remain clearly identified.
